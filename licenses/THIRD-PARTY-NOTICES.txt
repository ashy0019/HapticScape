Level 99 crowd cheer -- CC0 1.0
--------------------------------

The Level 99 ceremony includes an edited excerpt of "CRWDApls_Loud Applause,
Cheering, Whistling, Barking_ShaneVincent_GSC24_FUMA-AmbeoVR.wav", recorded
and edited by Shane Vincent (Freesound user ShangusBurger) during GameSoundCon
2024. The source recording was released under CC0 1.0. It was trimmed,
downmixed to mono, resampled, filtered, and faded for HapticScape.

The source and license details are supplied in LEVEL99-CHEER-CC0.txt.

Build-only dependencies
-----------------------

Lombok, JUnit, and Hamcrest are used only while compiling or testing
HapticScape. They are not included in the Windows client JAR. The packaging
build verifies that JUnit, Hamcrest, and HapticScape test classes are absent.
